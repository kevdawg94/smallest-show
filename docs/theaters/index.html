Smallest ShowFor theaters

If you run a cinema and see smallest-show in your logs, this is us: what we read from your site or ticketing platform, how often, and how to ask us to change it.

What Smallest Show is

A free iPhone widget that lists tonight's showtimes at independent cinemas across the US. Every showing links to the theater's own ticket page, and every theater's name to its own site, so the sale happens where it always does. It has no ads and no accounts, and sells nothing. The install page shows what it looks like.

What we read

Only the public schedule pages and feeds that your own website or your ticketing platform already serves to anyone who visits: the listings your customers see. Nothing behind a login, and no browser automation, just plain requests for the same pages and data a browser loads to show your schedule. Each read asks for the next seven days.

How often

  • A ticketing platform that shows what's sold out: five times a day, about every three hours from 9 am to 9 pm Pacific.
  • A theater's own website, or a platform whose pages don't show what's sold out: twice a day, around 6 am and 6 pm Eastern.

Both are an hour earlier in winter. Now and then, when we add a theater or fix how one is read, a person also checks its pages by hand, under the same name.

How politely

  • No more than two requests at a time to any one site, and no more than two theaters on the same platform read at once.
  • Every request asks for gzip, so a page comes over at a fraction of its size.
  • A refused request isn't retried. A server error or a timeout gets two more tries, a few seconds apart.
  • A 429, or a 503 with Retry-After: we wait the pause you ask for, up to a minute, and try once more. If you ask for longer, send a 429 with no pause, or push back again, we leave that site, and every theater on that platform, alone until the next scheduled read.
  • Every request names us. The User-Agent is: smallest-show/1.0 (independent cinema showtimes; https://smallest.show/theaters/)

What we keep and publish

For each theater, its name, where it is and a link to its site. For each film, its title, rating and running time, and a link to its page. For each showing, its time, whether it's sold out, the screen where you name one, and a link to its ticket page. Every listing carries the time it was read, and the widget says when one is out of date.

That's all. No posters or other images, no descriptions, no prices, no seat counts. The current week's listings are published as data files in the public repository the widget reads; nothing older is published there. We keep a private copy of what each read received, to test changes to our code against, and it isn't published.

A change, or leaving us out

If you'd like us to read a different page or feed, read less often, correct a listing, or leave your theater out, tell us and we'll do it. Leaving out means we stop reading your site and your theater drops out of the widget.

The way to reach us is an issue on github.com/kevdawg94/smallest-show. Say which theater and what you'd like, and we'll answer there. It takes a free GitHub account, and what you write there is public.

Smallest Show is made by Kevin Celustka and named for The Smallest Show on Earth (1957), where a young couple inherit a run-down cinema between two railway bridges. The showtimes are the theaters'; we only point to them.
