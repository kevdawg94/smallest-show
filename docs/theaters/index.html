If you run a cinema and see smallest-show in your logs, this is us: what we read from your site or ticketing platform, how often, and how to ask us to change it.

What Smallest Show is

A free iPhone widget that lists tonight's showtimes at independent cinemas across the US. Every showing links to the theater's own ticket page, and every theater's name to its own site, so the sale happens where it always does. It has no ads and no accounts, and sells nothing. The install page shows what it looks like.

What we read

Only the public schedule pages and feeds that your website or ticketing platform already serves to anyone: the listings your customers see. Nothing behind a login, and no browser automation.

How often

A ticketing platform, up to five times a day between 9 am and 9 pm Pacific. A theater's own website, twice a day, around 6 am and 6 pm Eastern.

Our approach

  • No more than two requests at a time to any one site, each asking for compressed pages, so a read is light.
  • We back off when asked: a refused request isn't retried, a 429 or Retry-After is honored, and a site that pushes back is left alone until the next scheduled read.
  • Every request names us, the scheduled reads and a person checking by hand alike: smallest-show/1.0 (independent cinema showtimes; https://smallest.show/theaters/)

If you'd like the details, such as exact times, request pacing, or what we do with each response, ask and we'll send them.

What we publish and keep

Your theater's name, where it is and a link to its site; each film's title, rating and running time; and each showing's time, whether it's sold out, and a link to its ticket page. No images, descriptions, prices or seat counts. Only the current week is published, as the data files the widget reads. We keep a private copy of each read, which isn't published.

A change, or leaving us out

If you'd like us to read a different page or feed, read less often, correct a listing, or leave your theater out, tell us and we'll do it.

Email us at info@smallest.show. Say which theater and what you'd like, and we'll reply.

The showtimes are the theaters'; we only point to them. About the project says why we read them at all.

Made by Kevin Celustka for his own Friday nights, and named for The Smallest Show on Earth (1957), where a young couple inherit a run-down cinema between two railway bridges.
