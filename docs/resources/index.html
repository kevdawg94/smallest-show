Others doing this work

The associations that support independent cinemas, and the guides that list what they show, in the US and around the world.

Supporting independent cinemas

The groups that speak for America's independent exhibitors, and a reader-kept register of the theaters themselves.

  • Art House Convergence

    United States

    The association of America's art house cinemas: an annual conference, research on audiences and operations, and Art House Theater Day.

  • Independent Cinema Alliance

    US and Canada

    About 180 independent exhibitors with about 5,000 screens between them, sharing vendor deals, marketing and industry news.

  • Cinema Treasures

    United States

    A register of America's movie theaters, open and closed, with their histories, kept by its readers.

City guides

The people who list their city's independent and repertory screenings, most of them by hand.

  • Screen Slate

    New York and the Bay Area

    The daily guide to repertory and independent screenings in New York since 2011, with a weekly Bay Area column since 2023. A nonprofit.

  • Revival Houses

    Los Angeles

    Repertory, art house and independent showtimes around Los Angeles, updated daily since 2017.

  • Cine-File

    Chicago

    A weekly, volunteer-run guide to Chicago's independent and repertory screenings.

  • Screen Boston

    Boston

    Repertory and independent screenings across the Boston area.

  • Philm Club

    Philadelphia

    A selective guide to what's playing in Philadelphia's cinemas.

  • PDX Movie Times

    Portland, Oregon

    Portland's independent theaters and their showtimes, in a weekly newsletter.

Around the world

The networks that fund and connect independent cinemas abroad, and one London guide.

  • Clusterflick

    London

    What's on across more than 500 London venues, with its data open for other guides to build on.

  • Independent Cinema Office

    United Kingdom

    The UK's national organization for independent film exhibition: advice, training and programming support for cinemas.

  • BFI Film Audience Network

    United Kingdom

    Eight film hubs, funded by the National Lottery, that build audiences for independent film across the UK.

  • Europa Cinemas

    Europe

    The EU-backed network of independent cinemas: 1,290 members in 39 countries.

  • AFCAE

    France

    The association of France's art et essai cinemas, the houses the national film agency recognizes for what they program.

  • CICAE

    International

    The International Confederation of Art Cinemas: a network of art house networks, and a bridge between festivals and cinemas.
