Put it on your iPhone

1. Get Scriptable free, on the App Store 2. Add to Scriptable the Smallest Show script

If you already have Scriptable, tap Add to Scriptable. There's nothing to edit, and Smallest Show updates itself.

On a computer, the buttons won't work. Open smallest.show/widget on your iPhone, or scan this code with its camera to open smallest.show.

Install

Four steps

  1. 1

    Get Scriptable

    Scriptable is a free app on the App Store. Smallest Show runs inside it.

    Get Scriptable
  2. 2

    Add the script

    Tap Add to Scriptable. Safari downloads SmallestShow.scriptable. Open it from the Downloads arrow in Safari or from the Files app. Scriptable asks to add the script. Tap Add.

    Add to Scriptable
  3. 3

    Pick your theaters

    Open Scriptable and tap Smallest Show to run it. Choose your city, then check the theaters you go to and close the list. To change them later, run it again and tap Theaters › at the top.

  4. 4

    Add the widget

    Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Scriptable and add the large size. Press and hold the new widget and tap Edit Widget. Set Script to Smallest Show and When Interacting to Run Script.

Using the widget. Tap a showtime for its ticket page, a film's title for its page, or a theater's name for its website. Tap anywhere else for the full list. Sold-out times are outlined. After the night's last screening, the widget turns to tomorrow.

Made by Kevin Celustka. Named for The Smallest Show on Earth (1957), in which a young couple inherit a run-down cinema between two railway bridges.

Free, with no accounts, ads or tracking.
